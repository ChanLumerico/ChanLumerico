Research interests

Three threads, one question: how a model builds an internal world and lets you intervene in it.

01

Interactive world models

Learned simulators that stay coherent under a user's actions, and what it takes to keep long-horizon rollouts stable.

02

Diffusion & flow models

Score matching, annealed Langevin dynamics, and the probabilistic scaffolding underneath modern generative vision.

03

Systems from scratch

Autodiff, computational graphs, and lightweight adaptation — implemented rather than imported, so the theory has somewhere to land.

Currently reading Lai et al., The Principles of Diffusion Models arXiv:2510.21890
